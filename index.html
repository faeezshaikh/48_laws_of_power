Lessons for the Investor

Read this first: Greene wrote about courts and conquest, not markets. The translation that works for investors is almost always defensive: master your own psychology, guard your reputation, and recognize when these tactics are being used on you — by promoters, management teams, and the market narrative machine.

Life Playbooks — the laws, role by role

Ethics note: with family, friends and community, these laws work best inverted — as awareness (spotting guilt-trips, triangulation and manipulation used on you) and as harmony tools (timing, tact, emotional deposits). Influence built on warmth and reliability outlasts influence built on tactics. In business negotiation, more of the playbook applies as written.
